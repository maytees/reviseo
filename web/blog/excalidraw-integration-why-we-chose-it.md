Excalidraw brings a friendly, approachable drawing experience that feels like pen on paper—ideal for pointing at UI elements without over‑engineering the process. Here’s why it’s perfect for Reviseo.

## Hand‑Drawn, Human, and Fast

- The rough, hand‑drawn style encourages quick, imperfect annotations—exactly what you want from clients.
- It’s lightweight and performs well across devices.
- The UX is intuitive: arrow, rectangle, freehand, text—no training required.

## Built for Context

Reviseo captures the current page, viewport, and a screenshot, then layers Excalidraw on top so annotations map precisely to what your client sees.

## Examples of Annotations

- Circle a misaligned element and add a note: “Align with heading.”
- Draw an arrow to a button: “Primary color; increase contrast.”
- Freehand box around a section: “Swap hero image to v2.”

![Annotation examples](/cdn/excalidraw-annotations.png)

## Why Not Just Use Loom or Email?

Loom is great for walkthroughs. Email is great for decisions. But neither lets you point at pixels and submit an actionable, trackable change request in seconds. Excalidraw inside Reviseo does.

If you design or build interfaces, this is the most natural way to collaborate.
