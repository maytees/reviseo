We’ve been in the trenches of freelance web work: late‑night revision rounds, contradictory emails, a dozen Loom links, and a final message that says, “Can you just make it pop?” We built Reviseo so those days don’t have to define your workflow.

## The Problem We Kept Running Into

- Vague emails: “Move this a bit” (move what?)
- Looms that take longer to watch than to fix
- Screenshots with red circles but no URL or context
- Feedback scattered across Slack, email, and comments

This chaos wastes time and creates risk. It’s how projects drift, scopes creep, and relationships strain.

## The Moment It Clicked

We realized every revision needed the same core pieces: the exact page and viewport, a screenshot, and a clear indication of what changed and why. If clients could point directly at the interface and write one actionable note, most of the confusion would vanish.

## What Reviseo Does Differently

- Visual annotations powered by a delightful, hand‑drawn feel
- Automatic context (URL, viewport, screenshots)
- A simple, status‑based workflow to move items to “Done”
- Works anywhere—static sites, WordPress, Shopify, Next.js, you name it

## Who It’s For

- Freelancers juggling multiple clients
- Small agencies trying to standardize quality
- Builders who want fewer meetings and faster cycles

## The Result

You get clarity. Clients feel heard. Work moves faster. And you keep your evenings.

If that resonates, try Reviseo on your next sprint. The difference shows up immediately.
